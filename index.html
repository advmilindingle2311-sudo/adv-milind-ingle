FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you consult.

A few practical answers to common questions about the consultation process.

Request Consultation
How do I request a consultation? +

You can use the consultation form below, contact WhatsApp directly or send an email with a brief description of the matter.

What should I bring for the first consultation? +

Keep relevant notices, FIR or complaint copies, court documents, agreements, identity documents and other important papers available where applicable.

Can I send documents on WhatsApp? +

You may first contact the office regarding the appropriate method of sharing documents. Avoid sending highly confidential material until the appropriate communication channel is confirmed.

Is legal advice available without a court case? +

Yes. Preliminary legal consultation, document review and drafting assistance may be considered depending on the nature of the matter.

Does contacting this website create an advocate-client relationship? +

No. A website enquiry or communication does not by itself create an advocate-client relationship. Formal engagement is subject to appropriate instructions and acceptance of the matter.
